tcg/wasm64: a same-page access reuses the previous TLB hit

Every qemu_ld/st paid its own inline TLB probe -- three dependent loads
(mask, table, comparator) and ~12 x64 before the addend load -- although
hot TBs spill and refill the guest stack in ldm/stm/push/pop clusters
whose words share a page: in the hottest J2ME blit TB 8 of its 10 stack
accesses do.

A hit that another access of the same kind (mmu_idx, load or store) and
the same address base follows now leaves the entry's page and addend in
two locals, and that next access tests its address against the page
instead of probing: ((addr ^ page) & (PAGE_MASK | a_mask)) == 0 for an
access that cannot straddle, (addr ^ page) < PAGE_SIZE - adj for an
unaligned one.  A miss of the probe resets the page to -1, which no
zero-extended address matches.  The memo is kept only across
straight-line code: any call (a helper may refill the TLB) or label (a
join) ends it at translation time.  A scan at TB start pairs the
accesses: same kind, and addresses derived from one (temp, version) by
moves and constant adds -- the words of an ldm (addr += 4), the fields
of one struct -- so an access through an unrelated register never pays
the test to miss it, and a lone access emits exactly what it did before.
An entry that hit is clean for its whole page, so the flags (MMIO,
NOTDIRTY, watchpoints) it was compared with hold for the second access
too.

Without the base pairing the memo hit 83 % of 173 k tests/Mi and lost
(J2ME +3.4 % +- 0.5): the 30 k misses/Mi paid the test, a mispredict
and then the probe.  With it: 130.6 k tests/Mi, 94 % hits.  CENSUS
