tcg: la_cross_call is a no-op on wasm64

liveness_pass_1 sweeps every temp at each call and at each memory op
(they carry TCG_OPF_CALL_CLOBBER) to strip the call-clobbered registers
from the temps' preferences.  The allocator frees the clobbered
registers at the call regardless, so the sweep is quality-only -- and
the wasm64 backend clobbers R0 and R1 alone, the last two in its
allocation order, with spills all but nonexistent, so it changed no
allocation while costing an estimated quarter of the pass (the pass was
4.4 % of a boot's first 12 s).  Registers are wasm locals here; a spill
would be a local too.

S75 boot, idlebench milestones, 4 runs per arm (slot 2 = tcg_reg_free
syncs on both arms): BOOT.  gate keep GREEN.
