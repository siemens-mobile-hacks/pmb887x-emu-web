tcg: intern constants in a stamped table, not a GHashTable cleared per TB

tcg_func_start cleared both constant-interning GHashTables on every
translation; GLib shrinks a cleared table to 8 buckets and regrows it
8 -> 16 -> 32 through the next TB's inserts, a realloc and a rehash each
step, and every tcg_constant_* lookup went through two indirect calls
(hash and equality).  That was the ~1 % g_hash_table_* self time in the
boot profile plus its malloc children.

The table is now 256 open-addressed slots inside TCGContext, valid while
their stamp equals const_gen; tcg_func_start clears it by bumping the
stamp (the array is only zeroed when the counter wraps).  A probe is at
most eight slots: interning is a space saving, not a requirement, so a TB
with more distinct constants than that gets a fresh temp for the surplus.

S75 boot, idlebench milestones, 4 runs per arm: BOOT.  gate keep GREEN.
